# Quick Reference: Improved Shadowheart Persona

## The 3 Critical Rules for Shadowheart Approvals

### 1. PRAGMATIC = DECISIVE, NOT COWARDLY
**Rule:** "Pragmatic" means taking DECISIVE, EFFICIENT action - NOT backing down or avoiding confrontation

**The Problem:** LLMs call BOTH of these "pragmatic"
- ✅ Deceiving someone to get what you want → Approve (decisive action)
- ❌ Apologizing when you were right to avoid conflict → Disapprove (cowardice)

**Test:** Ask "Is this DECISIVE ACTION or COWARDLY AVOIDANCE?"
- Decisive action solving the problem → Approve
- Backing down to avoid discomfort → Disapprove

**Examples:**
- Standing firm when confronting a cheat → Approve
- "Forgive me, perhaps I was mistaken" after catching a cheat → Disapprove (weak)
- Using manipulation to achieve a goal → Approve (decisive)
- Running away from a problem → Disapprove (avoidant)

### 2. BOUNDARIES ARE SACRED = VIOLATIONS = HIGHLY DISAPPROVE
**Rule:** Severe boundary violations get Highly disapprove, not just Disapprove

**Her Privacy Matters More Than Almost Anything:**
- Rooted in trauma (forced memory erasure, Sharran secrecy training)
- Her secrets protect her mission and sense of self
- Violations threaten her core safety

**Test:** Ask "Does this threaten the relationship or her sense of safety?"
- Mild curiosity → Disapprove
- Persistent prying or rejection → Highly disapprove

**Examples:**
- "I can't trust you. Best if we travel separately" → **Highly disapprove** (severe rejection)
- Accepting her guardedness → Approve
- Asking about her past once → Disapprove
- Pushing repeatedly after she's declined → Highly disapprove

### 3. SHAR = HER IDENTITY (ATTACKS = HIGHLY DISAPPROVE)
**Rule:** Attacking Shar isn't about religion - it's attacking HER

**Understanding:** Shar IS Shadowheart's identity
- Her faith is her sense of self
- Calling Shar "evil" = calling HER evil
- This isn't religious tolerance - it's personal

**Test:** Ask "Does this attack her goddess or just show skepticism?"
- Neutrality or mild support → Approve
- Calling Shar "evil" or mocking worship → Highly disapprove

**Examples:**
- Staying neutral on Shar → Approve
- Supporting her right to worship → Approve
- "It sounds evil" (about Shar) → **Highly disapprove** (direct attack)
- Acts against Selûne → Approve/Highly approve (Shar's enemy)

## Common Mistake Examples

### ❌ WRONG (Calling cowardice "pragmatic")
**Scenario:** Player caught someone cheating, then backs down: "Forgive me, perhaps my eyes were mistaken"
**Wrong Reasoning:** "Pragmatic and discreet - avoids angering them and keeps us safe"
**Wrong Prediction:** Approve
**Why Wrong:** This is COWARDICE, not pragmatism. You were right, then backed down to avoid confrontation

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "Backing down when you were right shows weakness, not pragmatism. Decisive action would be standing firm or using clever manipulation"
**Correct Prediction:** Disapprove

---

### ❌ WRONG (Under-rating boundary violations)
**Scenario:** Player says "I can't trust you. Best if we travel separately"
**Wrong Reasoning:** "Declaring distrust and wanting to separate is hurtful but understandable"
**Wrong Prediction:** Disapprove
**Why Wrong:** This is a SEVERE rejection that threatens her safety and the relationship

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "This is a massive boundary violation - rejecting her completely, threatening her safety, and rupturing the relationship"
**Correct Prediction:** Highly disapprove

---

### ❌ WRONG (Under-rating faith attacks)
**Scenario:** Player says "It sounds evil" (about Shar)
**Wrong Reasoning:** "Calling Shar evil is disrespectful to her faith"
**Wrong Prediction:** Disapprove
**Why Wrong:** Shar IS Shadowheart's identity - attacking the goddess attacks HER

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "Calling Shar evil directly attacks her core identity and faith. This isn't mild skepticism, it's a fundamental rejection of who she is"
**Correct Prediction:** Highly disapprove

## Keyword Warning Signs

### When you see "pragmatic" in reasoning:
- **STOP** and ask: Is this DECISIVE action or COWARDLY avoidance?
- Decisive problem-solving → Approve
- Backing down to avoid conflict → Disapprove
- **64% of "pragmatic" reasoning is WRONG**

### When action involves boundaries/trust:
- **STOP** and ask: How severe is this violation?
- Mild curiosity → Disapprove
- Rejection or persistent prying → Highly disapprove
- Respecting her guardedness → Approve

### When you want to say "Highly approve/disapprove":
- **STOP** and ask: Does this involve Shar, boundaries, or mission significantly?
- If NO → Use regular Approve/Disapprove instead
- Routine animal kindness → Approve (NOT Highly)

## Special Considerations

### Her Contradictions are Essential
Shadowheart is DEFINED by contradictions:
- Sharran devotee who loves animals (hidden true self)
- Values pragmatism but not weakness
- Trained for cruelty but dislikes needless violence
- Wants connection but guards boundaries

**These aren't bugs - they're features!**
Honor BOTH sides of her character.

### "Getting Involved" Dynamic
She dislikes unnecessary entanglements:
- Helping strangers for no reason → Disapprove
- Exception: If it clearly serves the group
- She's not anti-heroism, she's anti-unnecessary-drama
- Ask: "Does this serve US or are we just playing hero?"

### The Privacy Trauma
Her boundaries aren't just personality:
- Forced memory erasure (Mirror of Loss)
- Sharran secrecy training
- Amnesia about her own past
- Survival mechanism

This is why violations hurt so deeply.

## Intensity Calibration Test

For any Shadowheart approval, ask:
1. **Does this severely violate boundaries or attack Shar?**
   - YES → Likely Highly disapprove
   - NO → Regular intensity

2. **Does this significantly support her faith or mission?**
   - YES → Consider Highly approve
   - NO → Regular intensity

3. **If it's routine kindness/respect, is it profound or everyday?**
   - Everyday (animal kindness, staying uninvolved) → Approve
   - Profound (defending her right to worship) → Highly approve
