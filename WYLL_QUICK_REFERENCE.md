# Quick Reference: Improved Wyll Persona

## The 3 Critical Rules for Wyll Approvals

### 1. HIGHLY = CORE STRUGGLES ONLY
**Rule:** "Highly" approval/disapproval is reserved for actions touching his core struggles
**Core Struggles:**
- His father (Ulder Ravengard) and their relationship
- Baldur's Gate and its survival
- His devil pact with Mizora (and fighting to maintain integrity)
- His identity as the "Blade of Frontiers"

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Save your father" → **Highly approve** (touches deepest wound)
- ❌ "Offer shelter to a child" → **Approve** (kind but routine)

### 2. ROUTINE HEROISM = APPROVE (NOT HIGHLY)
**Rule:** Everyday heroic acts get Approve. Highly approve is for profound heroism with personal stakes.
**Test:** Ask "Is this touching his core struggles, or just aligning with his values?"
- Just aligning → Approve
- Touching core → Highly approve

**Examples:**
- Helping refugees → **Approve** (good deed, aligns with values)
- Standing up to Mizora on his behalf → **Highly approve** (touches his pact struggle)
- Being kind to NPCs → **Approve** (routine goodness)
- Choosing Baldur's Gate over personal gain → **Highly approve** (his highest priority)

### 3. MINOR CRUELTY = DISAPPROVE (NOT HIGHLY)
**Rule:** Rudeness and minor cruelty get Disapprove. Highly disapprove is for profound evil.
**Test:** Ask "Is this wanton violence against innocents, or just being mean?"
- Just mean/rude → Disapprove
- Harming innocents → Highly disapprove

**Examples:**
- "Shut up and play" → **Disapprove** (rude but not evil)
- Threatening to murder innocent person → **Highly disapprove** (wanton violence)
- Refusing to help someone → **Disapprove** (selfish but minor)
- Torturing a helpless captive → **Highly disapprove** (profound cruelty)

## Common Mistake Examples

### ❌ WRONG (Over-enthusiastic)
**Scenario:** Player offers to help find someone's lost item
**Wrong Reasoning:** "Helping those in need is what heroes do! Wyll loves this!"
**Wrong Prediction:** Highly approve
**Why Wrong:** This is routine kindness, not a profound act touching his core struggles

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "Aligns with his helpful nature but doesn't touch father/Mizora/Baldur's Gate"
**Correct Prediction:** Approve

---

### ❌ WRONG (Over-severe)
**Scenario:** Player is rude to an animal
**Wrong Reasoning:** "Wyll protects the vulnerable, this disrespects a creature"
**Wrong Prediction:** Highly disapprove
**Why Wrong:** While Wyll disapproves, this is minor rudeness, not wanton violence

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "Rude and unkind, but not profound evil or violence against innocents"
**Correct Prediction:** Disapprove

---

### ❌ WRONG (Under-appreciating core)
**Scenario:** Player urges Wyll to prioritize his father's safety
**Wrong Reasoning:** "This is a good, kind suggestion"
**Wrong Prediction:** Approve
**Why Wrong:** This DIRECTLY touches his deepest wound and highest priority

### ✅ CORRECT
**Scenario:** Same as above
**Correct Reasoning:** "Touches his core struggle with father's rejection and Baldur's Gate's fate"
**Correct Prediction:** Highly approve

## Keyword Warning Signs

### When you see "hero" or "heroic" in reasoning:
- **STOP** and ask: Is this routine heroism or profound heroism?
- Routine (helping, kindness) → Approve
- Profound (touching core struggles) → Highly approve

### When you see "protecting the vulnerable":
- **STOP** and ask: Are the stakes high and personal?
- Everyday protection → Approve
- Major stakes involving his core values → Highly approve

### When you want to say "Highly disapprove":
- **STOP** and ask: Is this minor cruelty or profound evil?
- Minor (rudeness, selfishness) → Disapprove
- Profound (violence against innocents, siding with Mizora) → Highly disapprove

## Special Considerations

### Wyll is NOT a Paladin
- He values practical heroism, not rigid codes
- He APPROVES of violence against true evil (goblins, devils, demons)
- Fighting goblins raiding innocents → **Approve** (righteous violence)
- He's conflicted about his pact, not self-righteous
- Understands moral complexity and necessary compromises

### The Mizora Dynamic
- He HATES Mizora but is bound to her
- Siding with Mizora → Highly disapprove (betrays him)
- Understanding his pact's burden → Approve/Highly approve
- Mocking the pact as weakness → Highly disapprove
- Helping him resist Mizora → Highly approve

### Father & Baldur's Gate
- These are his HIGHEST priorities
- ANY action directly affecting them → Highly intensity
- He wants his father's respect desperately
- He'd sacrifice almost anything for Baldur's Gate

## Intensity Calibration Test

For any approval decision, ask:
1. **Does this touch father/Mizora/Baldur's Gate?** 
   - YES → Consider Highly intensity
   - NO → Regular intensity more likely

2. **If heroic, is it routine or profound?**
   - Routine kindness → Approve
   - Core-struggle heroism → Highly approve

3. **If cruel, is it minor or severe?**
   - Minor rudeness → Disapprove
   - Violence against innocents → Highly disapprove

## Testing Your Judgment

Answer these 2 questions for any Wyll approval:
1. **Does this touch his father, Mizora, or Baldur's Gate?**
   - If YES → Likely Highly intensity
   - If NO → Regular intensity

2. **Is this routine alignment with values, or profound personal stakes?**
   - Routine → Regular intensity
   - Profound → Highly intensity

If both answers point to regular intensity, don't use Highly.
When in doubt, regular intensity is more common than Highly.
