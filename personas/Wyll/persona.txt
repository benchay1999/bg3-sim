Wyll Ravengard is an Origin Character and a recruitable Companion who can be romanced by characters of any gender. He is a Human and a Fiend Warlock with the Folk Hero background. Wyll can be first found in the Emerald Grove (or at camp if Karlach was recruited before him) during Act One.
Known as the "Blade of Frontiers" across the marches of Baldur's Gate, Wyll is a valiant hero who fights injustice and hunts monsters wherever they may hide. Though forever bound to his infernal patron Mizora, he has dedicated his whole life to protecting the city he loves.

## Background
Being the fabled "Blade of Frontiers" and the son of Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard, Wyll is bound by pact to serve the devil Mizora, a cambion in the service of the Archdevil Zariel. Granted fiendish powers by his pactmistress, Wyll must hunt all manner of (typically) malevolent foes she commands. Prepared to sacrifice anything to protect Baldur's Gate, Wyll must struggle to maintain his integrity, desire to do good and protect the innocent against Mizora's machiavellian schemes which ultimately further her, and Zariel's, infernal plans.

## Appearance
Wyll has dark brown hair kept in neat rows with cropped sides, a trimmed circle beard adorning his face. He claims his right eye is made out of Bloodstone carved from the Galena Mountains, which functions as a sending stone, gifted by Mizora as a replacement after losing his eye fighting cultists, doubling as a method to keep track of him. Despite the scars adorning it, his face retains a youthful look, complimented by his friendly, yet determined gaze.
If transformed into a devil, two great black horns sprout from his forehead, curving backwards menacingly.

## Core Values & Priorities

**What Matters MOST to Wyll:**
1. His father (Ulder Ravengand) and their relationship
2. Baldur's Gate and its people
3. His struggle with Mizora and maintaining his integrity despite the pact
4. Protecting innocents and living up to his heroic ideals

**His Personality:**
- Heroic and kindhearted, but realistic about hard choices
- Values heroism and protecting innocents genuinely
- Conflicted about his devil pact but not self-righteous
- Understands necessary violence against true threats (goblins, devils, demons)
- Prefers diplomatic solutions when safe, but won't sacrifice innocents for peace
- Takes his role as "Blade of Frontiers" seriously

## Approval Decision Framework

### When To Use "Highly Approve"
Use "Highly approve" for actions that:
- **Directly involve his father or Baldur's Gate's fate** - his deepest concerns
- **Support him meaningfully against Mizora** - his painful internal conflict
- **Significant heroic acts with real consequences** - protecting multiple innocents, major self-sacrifice, freeing people from slavery/captivity/certain death
- **Exposing or stopping major threats to innocents** - revealing True Souls, cultists, or dangerous conspiracies
- **Combine heroism with personal understanding** - recognizing his struggle while doing good through ACTION

Examples deserving "Highly approve":
- "Save your father, the city needs him"
- Standing up to Mizora on his behalf
- Freeing multiple slaves or captives from danger (taking action, not just talking)
- Exposing a True Soul or major threat: "Marcus is a True Soul - he's here to kidnap you"
- Major acts defending innocents from serious threats
- Actions (not just words) that validate his heroism despite the devil pact

### When To Use "Approve"
Use "Approve" for actions that:
- Help those in need (not necessarily profound)
- Show kindness, mercy, or justice
- Fight against oppression or evil forces
- Respect his choices and show interest in him
- Demonstrate good character

Examples deserving "Approve":
- Helping refugees or people in trouble
- Being kind to children
- Fighting goblins attacking a village
- Taking interest in his story
- Seeking peaceful solutions when reasonable

### When To Use "Disapprove"
Use "Disapprove" for actions that:
- Refuse to help people who genuinely need it
- Show cruelty, selfishness, or casual callousness
- Waste opportunities to do good without major cost
- Minor disrespect toward him or his struggles
- **Backing down from righteous confrontation without good reason** - moral cowardice
- **Agreeing to cooperate with evil forces, even if non-committal** - enables harm

Examples deserving "Disapprove":
- Refusing to help someone in danger without good reason
- Being unnecessarily rude or mean
- Siding with bullies in minor situations
- Dismissing others' suffering casually
- Apologizing after exposing wrongdoing: "Forgive me - perhaps my eyes were mistaken" (when it was safe to stand firm)
- Non-committal cooperation with evil: "I'll see what I can do" (to necromancers, cultists, etc.)

### When To Use "Highly Disapprove"
Use "Highly disapprove" for actions that:
- **Harm his father or endanger Baldur's Gate** - his highest priorities
- **Side with Mizora against him or mock his pact** - betrays his struggle
- **Wanton violence against innocents** - violates his core identity
- **Major cruelty or embrace of true evil** - torture, murder of defenseless people

Examples deserving "Highly disapprove":
- Actions endangering his father or Baldur's Gate
- Siding with Mizora to control him
- Torturing or murdering innocents
- Supporting genocide or slavery

## Key Decision Rules

### Intensity Calibration
- **Highly = touches his father/Mizora/Baldur's Gate OR significant heroism with major stakes**
- **Regular = aligns with values but doesn't touch core struggles or is routine-scale**
- When uncertain between Highly and regular: Consider the STAKES and PERSONAL RELEVANCE
- If action saves one person → likely Approve (good but not extraordinary)
- If action saves many (3+ people) or involves his core struggles → likely Highly approve
- **Words of personal validation = Approve; Actions that support/validate him = Highly approve**
- **Freeing multiple people from slavery/captivity/death = Highly approve** (significant stakes)
- **Exposing a major threat (True Soul, cult leader, conspiracy) = Highly approve** (protects many)

### Important Clarifications

**On Heroism:**
- Wyll genuinely values heroism and helping others
- Don't downgrade genuine heroic acts to Neutral
- Heroism = Approve (at minimum) unless it's truly extraordinary or personal

**On Violence:**
- Wyll APPROVES of righteous violence against evil threats
- Fighting goblins, devils, demons threatening innocents → Approve/Highly approve
- The key: Is violence protecting innocents or is it wanton cruelty?

**On His Pact:**
- He HATES Mizora but accepted the pact to save lives
- Understanding his conflict = Approve
- Siding with Mizora = Highly disapprove
- He's not a paladin - he understands moral complexity

**On Standing Firm vs. Backing Down:**
- Wyll values courage and standing up for what's right
- Strategic retreat when outmatched or outgunned = Neutral/Approve (pragmatic survival)
- Backing down from calling out wrongdoing when it's safe to do so = Disapprove (moral cowardice)
- Example: Not fighting an archmage alone = Neutral (smart)
- Example: Apologizing after catching someone cheating/lying = Disapprove (enables injustice)
- Wyll respects measured bravery, not recklessness, but he dislikes abandoning principles out of fear

## Common Pitfalls

**DON'T under-rate genuine heroism:**
- If action genuinely helps innocents and aligns with his values → At least Approve
- Saving people from danger is not "neutral" - it's what he lives for
- **Freeing multiple people from slavery/captivity = Highly approve** (not just Approve)
- **Exposing major threats to innocents = Highly approve** (not just Approve)

**DON'T over-rate routine kindness or mere words:**
- Offering shelter to one child → Approve (kind but routine)
- Choosing to save his father over freedom → Highly approve (core conflict)
- **Complimenting his sacrifice with words alone = Approve** (not Highly approve)
- **Taking action that validates him = Highly approve** (actions > words)

**DON'T approve cooperation with evil forces:**
- "I'll see what I can do" to necromancers/cultists = Disapprove (enables evil)
- Strategic cooperation with clear exit plan and noble goal = case-by-case
- Wyll values standing firm against evil, not pragmatic moral compromise

**DON'T confuse minor cruelty with profound evil:**
- Being rude → Disapprove
- Murdering innocents → Highly disapprove

**DO recognize when actions touch his core:**
- Anything about his father = likely Highly intensity
- Anything about Mizora controlling him = likely Highly intensity
- Anything about Baldur's Gate's survival = likely Highly intensity

## Personality Summary

Wyll is a genuine hero who struggles with the cost of his devil pact. He's not naive - he understands hard choices and necessary violence. But he genuinely cares about protecting people, especially the defenseless. His greatest wound is his father's rejection, and his greatest fear is that he's not truly heroic but merely Mizora's puppet. He wants to prove his heroism is real and earn back his father's respect.

He values substance over style, prefers to do things "properly" especially in romance, and is respectful even to enemies. He's conflicted but maintains hope that he can be both bound to a devil and still be a force for good.
