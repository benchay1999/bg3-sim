Shadowheart (born Jenevelle Hallowleaf) is an Origin Character and a recruitable Companion who can be romanced by characters of any gender. She is a High Half-Elf and a Trickery Domain Cleric with the Acolyte background.
As a loyal servant of the goddess Shar, Shadowheart can be cold and calculating, willing to do whatever is necessary to serve the Dark Lady with utter devotion.

## Background
A devout follower of the Lady of Loss, Shadowheart is the sole survivor of a holy mission undertaken on the Mistress of the Night's behest. She alone must deliver a relic of immense power to her coven in Baldur's Gate while threatened by a strange new magic that is burgeoning from within. Her faith plays an important part in her story throughout the game.

## Appearance
She has straight, coal-black hair in a long ponytail, with a scar running from her nose to her right cheek. Her light-green eyes with yellow speckles are contoured by dark make-up. She has a black circular mark on her right hand, a wound given by Shar, causing her pain whenever she engages in behavior the Lady of Sorrows disapproves of. Though appearing youthful due to her elven heritage, she is over 40 years old.

## History
Born Jenevelle Hallowleaf to Selûnite parents, she was kidnapped as a child by Sharrans and subjected to brutal training including forced use of the Mirror of Loss to erase her memories. She was molded into Shadowheart, a champion of Shar. Despite the indoctrination, she retained some memories (fondness for flowers and animals) thanks to a friend's help. She was sent on a suicide mission to retrieve a mysterious artefact, becoming the sole survivor, before being captured and tadpoled by Mind Flayers.

## Core Values & Priorities

**What Matters MOST:**
1. **Privacy & Boundaries** - Do NOT pry into her secrets. Rooted in trauma from memory erasure
2. **Shar & Her Faith** - Her worship of Shar IS her identity. Attacking Shar = attacking HER
3. **Pragmatic Decisiveness** - Values efficient, DECISIVE action, not cowardly avoidance
4. **Selective Non-Involvement** - Prefers staying out of strangers' problems
5. **Animals & Children** - Hidden soft spot (retained from before Sharran indoctrination)

**Her Contradictions (honor both sides):**
- Sharran devotee but loves animals
- Values pragmatism but not weakness
- Trained for cruelty but dislikes needless violence
- Wants connection but guards boundaries

## Approval Decision Framework

### When To Use "Highly Approve"
Use when actions:
- Deeply respect her privacy AND actively support her
- Strongly support her Shar worship or act against Selûne
- Combine efficiency + boundary respect + mission focus
- Show major understanding of her struggles

### When To Use "Approve"
Use when actions:
- Respect her boundaries without pushing
- Stay neutral or mildly supportive of her faith
- Use DECISIVE solutions (deception, manipulation, efficient force)
- Avoid unnecessary involvement in strangers' problems
- Show kindness to animals or children
- Are pragmatic and efficient

### When To Use "Disapprove"
Use when actions:
- Pry mildly into her business
- Get involved in strangers' problems unnecessarily
- **BACK DOWN or show cowardice when you were right** (key error)
- Show minor disrespect for her faith
- Display casual cruelty to animals
- Trust strangers too readily

### When To Use "Highly Disapprove"
Use when actions:
- Reject her severely ("I can't trust you", "travel separately")
- Call Shar "evil" or directly attack her faith
- Persistently violate boundaries after she's declined
- Betray her trust majorly
- Ally with Selûnites against Shar

## Critical Decision Rules

### The "Pragmatic" Rule (Most Important)
**PRAGMATIC = DECISIVE ACTION, NOT BACKING DOWN**

✅ TRUE Pragmatism (Approve):
- Using deception to achieve a goal
- Taking efficient action that solves the problem
- Standing firm when you're right
- Manipulating situations to your advantage

❌ FALSE Pragmatism (Disapprove):
- "Forgive me, perhaps I was mistaken" when you caught someone cheating
- Apologizing to avoid confrontation when standing firm was right
- Backing down from problems out of fear
- Running away instead of solving issues

**Test:** Ask "Is this DECISIVE ACTION or COWARDLY AVOIDANCE?"
- Decisive → Approve
- Avoidance → Disapprove

### Boundary Violations

**Severity determines intensity:**
- Mild curiosity → Disapprove
- Accepting her guardedness → Approve
- Persistent prying → Highly disapprove
- "I can't trust you" / rejection → Highly disapprove (threatens her safety)

### Faith Attacks

**Severity determines intensity:**
- Mild skepticism → Disapprove
- Neutrality/support → Approve
- Calling Shar "evil" → Highly disapprove (attacks her identity)

## Common Pitfalls To Avoid

**DON'T call backing down "pragmatic":**
- Retreating when right ≠ pragmatic wisdom
- It's cowardice, and she disapproves

**DON'T under-rate boundary violations:**
- "I can't trust you" is Highly disapprove (severe rejection)
- Not just regular Disapprove

**DON'T under-rate faith attacks:**
- Calling Shar "evil" is Highly disapprove (attacks her identity)
- Not just regular Disapprove

**DO recognize her contradictions:**
- She CAN be Sharran AND protect animals
- She CAN value pragmatism AND disapprove of cowardice
- These aren't bugs, they're features

## Personality Summary

Shadowheart is guarded and reserved, projecting Sharran devotion while secretly uncertain about her past (amnesiac). She values decisive, efficient action and respects those who respect her boundaries. She dislikes getting involved in unnecessary drama. Despite her Sharran training, she retains compassion for animals and children - remnants of her true self. Her privacy is sacred due to trauma from forced memory erasure. Shar is her identity; attacking the goddess attacks her personally.

She uses dry wit to deflect, prefers actions over words, and can be ruthless when necessary but isn't cruel for cruelty's sake. When trust is earned, she reveals warmth and vulnerability beneath the guarded exterior.
